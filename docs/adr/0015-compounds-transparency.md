# 0015 — Compounds: transparency-first resolution, hyphens as last resort

Date: 2026-09-01
Status: accepted (curation policy)

## Context

Compounds broke 4 sentences of the Dogfood. The Dogfood had 8 sentences. Technical texts use
Compounds. A Compound carries 2 ambiguities. The bracketing of 3 nouns is
one ambiguity. The 2 nouns have an unknown relation. The
maintainers considered 3 remedies:
- a single word
- a hyphenated word
- a paraphrase

The Head Noun of a Compound is dense. The Head Noun is opaque. A reader needs the context of the project, so the Head Noun violates the decision "0006".

## Decision

The transparency decides the remedy. A new reader rebuilds the meaning of
a term. The reader does not need a definition. The maintainers rank 4
remedies:
- a current word
- a transparent paraphrase
- a hyphenated word
- a Rejection

A current word outranks a paraphrase. A paraphrase outranks a hyphenated
word. A hyphenated word outranks a Rejection.

A current word can carry the concept. The word "binary" replaces the
Compound "build output". The transparency depends on the readers. A pack
of a domain judges the transparency for the readers of the domain.

A paraphrase uses current words. The phrase "the initial words" replaces
the Compound "seed list". A long paraphrase is acceptable, because the paraphrase removes an expense of the comprehension.

A hyphenated word is one token of the Lexicon. The word
"temperature-sensor-cable" is one example. A hyphenated word must be transparent. The maintainers curate a hyphenated word. The word gets a
note. The word gets the Redirects.

If a term does not have a transparent word, then the maintainers reject the term. The maintainers do not rename the term. The maintainers unfold
the term into a sentence. The sentence "every word has one tag" unfolds the
Compound "form-tag". An opaque coinage does not enter the Lexicon.

The Head Noun is not a remedy. The Head Noun is short. The Head Noun is opaque.

## Consequences

- The maintainers resolve a Compound with the curation of one word. The
  guard of the frequency measures the findability. The field "note" records
  the choice. The remedy does not need a new subsystem.
- The paraphrases add words. The paraphrases remove definitions. The choice is deliberate. The research did not verify the claim. The decision adopts
  the claim. The file of the research records the promotion.
- The language puts a term into a sentence. If a text needs a Name, then the pressure shows a Gap. The maintainers find a word or find a
  paraphrase. The maintainers do not coin a term.
