# 0043 — Idiomatic Structure (new policy); "never" is the first case

Date: 2026-09-04
Status: proposed (tentative). Extends ADR 0042.

## Context

The decision "0042" banned every word of the possibility. The word
"not" marks one case. The word "never" marks the history of the
cases. The 2 words do not have one meaning.

A Redirect of the word "never" removes the difference of the meaning,
so the decision rejects a Redirect. The word is not a Ban, because a
writer needs the meaning of the word.

## Decision

The decision names a new Category. The Category is the Idiomatic
Structure. An Idiomatic Structure names one pattern for a word. The pattern names
the meaning of the word. The word does not carry the meaning without
the pattern. The word does not appear without the pattern.

The word "never" takes the position of the word "not". The Category
"NEG" covers the word "not" and covers the word "never". The Grammar
does not need a new position for the word "never".

The word "never" needs the word "do", so the phrase matches the
phrase of the word "not". A writer says "the agent does never save
the report."

## Consequences

- A writer marks the history of the cases with one word.
- The word "never" is an Idiomatic Structure of the decision "0043".
- The decision keeps 4 words of the Gap:
  - "yet"
  - "still"
  - "already"
  - "later"
- The maintainers revisit every word of the Gap.
