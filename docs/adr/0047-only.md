# 0047 — "only": confined inside the Noun Phrase

Date: 2026-09-04
Status: proposed (tentative). Extends ADR 0043.

## Context

The word "only" was a Ban of the queue. The word "only" needs a Noun
Phrase. The word "only" causes a Scope Ambiguity in English.

The decision "0047" resolves the word "only".

## Decision

The word "only" is an Idiomatic Structure of the Noun Phrase. The
Grammar holds the word "only" inside the Noun Phrase. The Grammar
bans the word "only" before the predicate.

A writer marks the subject. A writer says "only the mechanism
stores the report". A writer marks the object. A writer says "the
mechanism stores only the report". A writer cannot build the
sentence "the mechanism only stores the report".

The scope of the word "only" is the Noun Phrase, and the scope is
never ambiguous.

## Consequences

- A writer names 2 shapes of a sentence. The 2 shapes do not
  collide.
- The decision resolves the last item of the queue.
- The Grammar does not need a new position, because the Noun Phrase
  already covers every position of a writer.
