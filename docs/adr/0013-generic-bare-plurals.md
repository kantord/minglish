# 0013 — Genericity: bare plurals, generic-only by fiat

Date: 2026-09-01
Status: proposed (tentative)

## Context

The Dogfood of the decision "0001" found a loss. The mandatory determiner
removed the generic statement of English, so the sentence "humans miss
checks" had 2 renderings. The rendering "the people miss the checks" is a
false definite. The rendering "a person misses a check" is ambiguous. The 2
renderings change the claim of the original sentence. A person catches the
loss. No metric catches the loss. The research of the project mentioned the
problem before the Dogfood.

## Decision

A Bare Plural is legal and has one fiat Parse. A Bare Plural does not have
a determiner. The noun can carry 2 Modifiers:
- an adjective
- a phrase of the Noun Preposition

The fiat Parse is a generic statement, so the sentence describes the kind.
The decision gives 2 examples:
- "machines find collisions"
- "agents retry requests"

English gives a second Parse to a Bare Plural. The sentence "dogs are
barking" has the existential Parse of English. The language bans the
existential Parse. A future decision introduces the word "some" for an
existential statement.

A generic statement is not a universal statement, because the sentence
"birds fly" tolerates an exception. The word "every" does not tolerate an
exception. A future decision introduces the word "every" for a universal
statement. The 2 Sentence Shapes carry 2 distinct meanings, so the language
keeps 2 distinct Sentence Shapes.

A singular noun needs a determiner, so a writer restructures a generic
statement into a Bare Plural.

The mandatory determiner gets one exception. The determiner prevents an
ambiguity. The Bare Plural has one fiat Parse, so the exception keeps the
purpose of the determiner.

A Bare Plural can take 4 positions:
- the subject
- the object
- the object of a Verb Preposition
- the Complement

A Bare Plural cannot follow the Noun Preposition. The Ban is a provisional
bound. If the evidence grows, then the maintainers revisit the bound.

## Consequences

- Policies use generic statements. Decisions use generic statements.
  Documents use generic statements. The language can say a generic
  statement, so the translation of the prose keeps the meaning. The Dogfood
  removed 2 Translation Pairs from the corpus. The maintainers return the 2
  Translation Pairs to the corpus, because the generic translation keeps
  the claim.
- The rule does not add a token, because a Bare Plural is shorter than a
  false definite. The decision "0006" prefers the short formulation.
- A writer must learn one fiat rule. A Bare Plural is a generic statement
  and is not an existential statement.
