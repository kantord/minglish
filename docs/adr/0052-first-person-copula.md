# 0052 — am: the Copula after "I"

Date: 2026-09-05
Status: proposed (tentative). Extends ADR 0003.

## Context

The Copula has 2 present Surface Forms. the word "I" does not match
the word "is" in English. the word "was" already matches the word
"I". the decision does not add a past Surface Form for the word "I".

a writer can write a report of one person with the word "am".

## Decision

the decision adds the word "am". the word "am" has the Form Tag
"COP_1SG". the word "I" opens the word "am".

the Grammar does not allow the word "am" after a common noun. a
common noun does not take the word "am".

## Consequences

- the language allows 4 Pronouns, and the word "I" is one Pronoun.
- a future decision can ban the word "I" in one document.
