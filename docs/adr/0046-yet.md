# 0046 — "yet": a negative-polarity Idiomatic Structure

Date: 2026-09-04
Status: proposed (tentative). Extends ADR 0043; resolves the last of
ADR 0044's deferred words.

## Context

The decision "0045" keeps the word "yet". The word "yet" needs the
word "not". English bans the phrase "the file yet exists". English
allows the phrase "the file does not yet exist".

## Decision

The Grammar of the decision "0046" repeats the rule of English. The
word "yet" follows the word "not" in every rule of the Grammar. The
Grammar does not have a rule of the word "yet" without the word
"not". A writer cannot build the sentence "the agent yet saves the
report".

## Consequences

- The Linter does not need the rule, because the Grammar already
  bans the shape.
- A writer marks the word "not" and marks the word "yet", and the
  writer names an expectation.
- The decision "0046" resolves the last word of the Gap.
