# 0045 — "afterward": the bare twin of "after"

Date: 2026-09-04
Status: proposed (tentative). Extends ADR 0043; resolves one of ADR
0044's 2 remaining words.

## Context

The decision "0044" keeps: the word "yet" and the word "later".

The decision "0045" resolves the word "later". The word "later" is
an adjective of the decision "0029". The decision avoids the
Collision, and the decision chooses the word "afterward".

## Decision

The word "afterward" is an Idiomatic Structure of the position
"PPv". The position "PPv" already covers the word "after". The word
"after" needs a Noun Phrase, and the Noun Phrase names the event.
The word "afterward" does not need a Noun Phrase.

The Grammar adds one alternative of the position "PPv". The Grammar
does not need a new position.

## Consequences

- A writer marks a time after an event, and the writer does not
  name the event.
- A writer keeps the adjective "later" for the old usage. A writer
  avoids the Collision.
- The decision keeps the word "yet". The word is a Gap, and the word
  needs a different position.
