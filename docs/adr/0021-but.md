# 0021 — but: adversative coordination enabled

Date: 2026-09-01
Status: proposed (tentative). Revises ADR 0004's deferral.

## Context

The decision "0004" deferred the word "but". The decision saw a decoration
in the word and did not see a claim. The sweep of the decisions met the word
"but" in 3 places, so the maintainers revisited the word. The word "but"
marks a contrast. The first Conjunct creates an expectation in the reader.
The second Conjunct contradicts the expectation. The contrast helps the
comprehension of the reader, so the contrast is not a decoration. The
decision "0006" protects the comprehension of the reader. The structure of
the word "but" is safe. The word does not refer to a thing and does not add
a scope to a Coordination.

## Decision

The word "but" has the Form Tag "CONJ" and has one fiat meaning. The word
"but" builds an adversative Coordination. The maintainers keep the rules of
a Coordination, so the rules cover the word "but". A Coordination joins 2
phrases of one Category. The rules of the decision "0004" cover the
Modifiers of a Conjunct, so the rules cover a Coordination with the word
"but".

The word "But" cannot open a sentence, because the word "but" joins 2
clauses inside one sentence. The rule protects the First Token, because the
First Token announces the Sentence Shape. A conjunction does not open a
Sentence Shape, so the construction of the Grammar enforces the rule.

The word "but" is a preposition in English. A writer cannot write the phrase
"all but one", because the word "all" is a Ban.

## Consequences

- A writer can mark a contrast, so the writer does not need a paraphrase. A
  paraphrase of a contrast flattens the meaning.
- The prior conjunctions join 2 claims and do not add a signal. The word
  "but" marks a contrast, so the word "but" is the first conjunction with a
  signal. The signal does not add an ambiguity, so the maintainers accept
  the word "but".
