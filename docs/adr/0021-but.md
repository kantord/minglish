## Decision

meaning. The meaning is an adversative Coordination. The rules of a
Coordination cover the word "but". A Coordination joins 2 phrases. A
Coordination joins 2 phrases of one Category. The rules of the decision
"0004" cover the Modifiers.

The word "But" cannot open a sentence. The word "but" joins 2 clauses
inside one sentence. The rule keeps the First Token. The Grammar enforces
the rule. A conjunction does not open a Sentence Shape.

The language cannot say the phrase "all but one", because the word "all" is
a Ban.
