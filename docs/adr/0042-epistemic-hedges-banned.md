# 0042 — Epistemic hedges are banned

Date: 2026-09-04
Status: proposed (tentative). Revises ADR 0009 (the Gap of "might" and
"could" becomes a Ban).

## Context

The decision "0006" prefers a precise text. A word can mark the
possibility of a claim. The word does not name the truth of the claim.

The decision "0009" banned the word "may". The decision "0009"
deferred: the word "might" and the word "could". A word of the
possibility does not have a clear Redirect, so the decision "0008"
makes a Ban.

The reader loses the fact, and the reader does not trust the writer.

## Decision

The decision bans 11 words of the possibility:
- "maybe"
- "perhaps"
- "probably"
- "possibly"
- "likely"
- "unlikely"
- "presumably"
- "apparently"
- "arguably"
- "somewhat"
- "seem"

The decision "0042" bans: the word "might" and the word "could".

A writer restructures the sentence. A writer names the source of the
claim, or a writer keeps the fact.

## Consequences

- The decision bans every word of the possibility.
- A writer restructures the sentence.
- The Linter shows the advice of the Ban for every word of the list.
