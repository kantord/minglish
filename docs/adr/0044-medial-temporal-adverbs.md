# 0044 — Medial temporal adverbs: "still" and "already"

Date: 2026-09-04
Status: proposed (tentative). Extends ADR 0043.

## Context

The decision "0043" keeps: the word "still" and the word "already".
The decision "0044" resolves: the word "still" and the word "already".

## Decision

The Idiomatic Structure covers 4 shapes of the predicate:
- the plain verb
- the Auxiliary "do"
- the modal
- the Copula

The position of the word stays before every shape. The shape of the
Copula reads "the file already is old". English does not prefer the
shape. English prefers the shape "the file is already old". The
decision "0044" made an identical choice to the decision "0043". The
decision accepts the expense.

If the writer uses the word, then the Grammar changes the predicate.
The Grammar does not change the predicate of an old sentence.

## Consequences

- A writer marks the continuation of a state with the word "still".
- A writer marks a complete state with the word "already".
- The decision "0044" keeps: the word "yet" and the word "later". The
  2 words need a different position.
