# 0050 — 3-way clause Coordination (Oxford-comma, different subjects)

Date: 2026-09-05
Status: proposed (tentative). Extends ADR 0037.

## Context

the decision "0037" names 2 shapes of a Coordination. the first shape
joins 2 predicates and does not need a comma. the second shape joins 2
clauses and needs one comma. the second shape does not join 3 clauses.

the word "if" opens a Conditional. the word "do" opens a Prohibition.
the word "no" opens a universal Negation. the old Grammar rejects one
sentence of the 3 clauses, so the writer writes 3 sentences.

the report "docs/judge-report.md" names the problem. 3 sentences have
one pattern, so the run is mechanical. the pattern appears in the
report.

## Decision

the Grammar allows a Coordination of 3 clauses. the first clause takes
a comma. the second clause takes a comma. the conjunction opens the
third clause. the comma of the third clause is mandatory.

the Grammar does not allow 4 clauses. the decision defers 4 clauses.
the decision does not have the evidence for 4 clauses.

## Consequences

- a writer joins 3 short clauses of one pattern into one sentence.
- the decision adds a shape and does not remove a shape.
- the decision "0038" still joins 2 clauses.
- a future decision can add 4 clauses with the evidence.
