# 0039 — without: a Verb Preposition for an absence

Date: 2026-09-04
Status: proposed (tentative). Extends ADR 0011.

## Context

A writer needed the word "without". A writer restructures the sentence
with a Negation. "the tool Lexgen rejects a group without examples" is
one example.

## Decision

The word "without" attaches to the verb, because the word "with"
attaches to the verb. The word "without" takes the Form Tag "PREP_V".
The word "without" keeps the bound of the decision "0011". The bound of
the decision "0011" allows one Verb Preposition on one clause.

## Consequences

- A writer can mark an absent thing, and the writer avoids a Negation.
